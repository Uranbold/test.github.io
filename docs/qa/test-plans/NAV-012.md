## 1. Scope

In scope: all 52 AC at the level the JVM/Robolectric environment allows: notification content, states and posting
policy; voice action; swipe-away; the restore record (write, heartbeat, delete, expiry, loop limit, schema mismatch,
privacy scan) and the restore through the **real** Ferrostar core on the host JVM (on the route, off the route, no fix,
standing still on a self-adjacent route); the AC 22 restart table and fallback notification; the lock-screen gate
flags; calls and the catch-up prompt; audio output rules; the battery hint rules and settings row; the sun function
against the official NOAA calculator; the merged manifest, strings/glossary and static checks; the NAV-005 suite as
regression with the named adaptations (AC 49).

Out of scope (story): iOS (NAV-015), Android Auto/CarPlay, waking the screen before manoeuvres, voice over HFP/SCO,
route trimming and manoeuvre arrows, R8 rules (architect tech-debt item), release.

**Not testable here (AC 52, §6):** anything that needs a real phone: notification rendering on OEM skins, the keyguard,
real process kills and the sticky restart per API level, OEM battery savers, Bluetooth/A2DP and first-word clipping,
cellular and VoIP calls, ducking/podcast resume, a real sunset, battery and cold-weather behaviour.
