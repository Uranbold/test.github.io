## 1. Scope

In scope: all 47 AC at the level the JVM/Robolectric environment allows; the «Дугуй» replays on QA stand-in tracks;
the NAV-005 regression suite; the static/resource checks of NAV-005 (strings, glossary, hosts, IPs); `npm test` in
`web/` unchanged (AC 5, 41).

Out of scope (story): iOS (NAV-015), origin choice and the turn list in the Android preview, switching alternatives
during guidance, NAV-003/NAV-004 web behaviour changes.

**Not testable here (AC 47, real Android phone; the PO's phone is an iPhone):** typing lock in a moving car/bus with
real GPS, OEM keyboards, line hit area with gloves, sheet dragging, on-device render times, «Дугуй» voice
audibility, TalkBack on a device, battery cost of 1 Hz fixes. **Not testable in JVM:** anything that needs MapLibre's
native library (drawn alternative lines, the 48 dp `queryRenderedFeatures` box, camera fit/zoom cap on a real map).
