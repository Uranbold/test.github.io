## 1. Scope

In scope: every AC at the level this environment allows: generator (stub + fake server), pack format and private
artifact, the D95 coordinated change on Android and web (rounding, spoken layer, segment output, golden set), the JVM
domain and duration checks, Android playback logic (Robolectric, fake clock, fake decoder, fake TTS), the fallback,
build variants and gates, repository and APK scans, and the cross-team interop of task breakdown §3.1 (a pack made by the
generator is accepted by the Android validator, so both sides compute the same canonical inventory and hashes).

Out of scope (story): iOS (NAV-015), web clip playback, an English pack, a voice setting, spoken street names.

**Not testable here (AC 57, recorded as not verified in every report):**
- a real Chimege run (304 requests), the real pack size, real audio quality and join prosody (D87)
- real-device playback: join silence, measured durations, clicks (AC 33), validation time ≤ 1 s (AC 39), Bluetooth
  wake-up clipping (edge cases), audio focus and ducking on a real phone
- `connectedDebugAndroidTest` (`ClipDecoderInstrumentedTest`, AC 15 on the real `MediaCodec`): needs an emulator or a
  device; none in the agent environment
- the PO's per-segment listen (AC 17, AC 53, D230) and phone check (AC 56); NAV-007 AC 9–11 (NAV-007's)
- the release checklist (AC 53: Chimege written terms, NAV-007 sign-off)

## 4. Interop procedure (task breakdown §3.1)
1. `make -C backend voice-pack-stub OUT=<scratch>/voice-pack-stub` (reference image). If the pack already exists, the
   generator exits 6; the existing pack is used after checking its manifest SHA-256 against the backend's recorded value,
   or a fresh pack is built into a new directory and compared byte for byte (AC 13).
2. `make -C backend voice-pack-verify DIR=<pack> POLICY=debug` → OK; `POLICY=release` → `STUB_NOT_ALLOWED` (expected).
3. `python3 -I tests/android/nav016/qa_nav016_checks.py inventory --manifest <pack>/manifest.json` → QA's hash = manifest.
4. `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest -Pnav.hostFerrostar=required -Pnav.voicePackDir=<pack>`: the whole suite, with
   `interopWithTheGeneratorPack` **executed (not skipped)** and `VoiceDurationCheck` reading the generator manifest
   (`longest-prompts.source.txt` names the pack).
5. `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug -Pnav.voicePackDir=<pack>` and without the property; AC 51 size delta; L7 `scan --apk`.
6. Record the pack triple (`voiceId`, `packVersion`, manifest SHA-256) in the report (pack-format §9).

## 5. Known coverage gaps (to be closed by the code owner; tracked in the reports)
- **G1 (AC 35):** only `stop()` is exercised with a pack prompt playing. Mute, the 3 s drop, off-route interruption,
  language switch and the phone-call catch-up are tested on the chime/TTS path only; they share `VoiceOutput.stop()` and
  the scheduler, so the risk is low, but no test plays a pack prompt and then triggers each rule.
- **G2 (AC 39):** no test holds the pack state at `Pending` (a non-running executor) and asserts the depart prompt waits
  ≤ 2 s and then chimes without A1.
- **G3 (AC 40):** no test simulates a restore (new `VoicePackSource`, `Pending`) and asserts re-validation with 0 network
  before the next prompt plays from the pack.

## 6. Entry and exit criteria
- **Entry:** generator commit, Android and web commits on the branch; the generator reference image available (or
  rebuildable offline); the D95 preconditions met (D232).
- **Exit (internal PO build, D108):** L1–L7 green; golden regenerated and reviewed; no open blocker or major defect;
  device and PO items listed as not verified.
- **Exit (public release):** additionally AC 53, AC 56 and NAV-007 AC 9–11 evidence, and `connectedDebugAndroidTest`
  on at least one device/emulator.
