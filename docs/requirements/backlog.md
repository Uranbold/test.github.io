## Business risks tracked across stories (data quality)
- OSM address coverage in UB (`addr:*`, khoroo, ger-district plots) affects search and reverse (NAV-001 R9, NAV-003).
- `name:mn` / `name:en` coverage affects Latin search and English UI (NAV-001 R6).
- `maxspeed` coverage affects ETA quality (NAV-001 R8). Traffic comes in Phase 3.
- Valhalla `mn-MN` narrative quality affects voice/banner text (NAV-001 R7, NAV-005, NAV-007). The BA desk check of 3.9.0 found likely defects: English strings in `enter_roundabout_verbal`, a broken placeholder, a typo, a zero-width space, a wrong verb, and left/east ambiguity (NAV-007 F1–F10).
- Mongolian TTS voice availability on Android/iOS devices is unknown (NAV-007 AC 9, NAV-005). It is a device and platform risk, not an OSM risk. *2026-10-01:* the NAV-017 real-iPhone check (AC 48) records whether iPhone Safari finds a Mongolian voice, as input for NAV-007 AC 9 and the voice spike (D75). *2026-10-01 (F2, [D82](decisions.md)):* eSpeak NG has Mongolian since PR #2202 (2025-05-27), so whether an `mn`-capable eSpeak NG build installs on Android is now an **open** NAV-007 AC 9 check on every team-owned Android matrix device, not an expected "no". A user-installed eSpeak NG `mn` voice would be used by NAV-005 AC 38 until NAV-016 ships (NAV-007 R11, [D89](decisions.md), [D91](decisions.md)).
- `turn:lanes` / `destination` density affects lane guidance (Phase 2).
- *2026-10-04 (NAV-006):* the daily rebuild brings OSM vandalism or broken edits to users within about 24 h. Large shrinks and damage to the reference routes are caught, small damage is not, and the fix is a one-step rollback plus a correction by a human mapper (NAV-006 R3). Fresh data does not improve address, `maxspeed` or lane coverage, but local mappers' fixes appear the next day (NAV-006 R5).
- *2026-10-04 (offline, [D163–D168](decisions.md)):* offline results are older than online ones: routing and search up to about 7 days (weekly), the map up to about one month (monthly, D166), so a new road can be routable offline before it is drawn on the offline map. Offline search quality depends on OSM name coverage (`name`, `name:mn`, `name:en`) and on countryside soum and bag names, which the NAV-003 golden set barely covers (hence the held-out query set). Offline search has no category matching beyond a small synonym table, so category queries may fail (D168).
- Once staging is public (NAV-008), outside testers will see all of the above data gaps directly. Tell testers what is known to be weak (NAV-008 R7).
