# Glossary (owner: business-analyst)

> **This glossary is binding for all agents.** It fixes **one** approved Mongolian term per concept. Every agent must use these terms in UI copy, voice prompts, banner text, resource files (`mn`, `en`), screen specs, docs and tests.
>
> - **Missing term?** Don't invent one in your own area. Add it under `requests_to_other_agents` → `business-analyst`, and the BA adds it here first.
> - **Status.** No Mongolian term below is final until the native-speaker panel under [NAV-007](stories/NAV-007-mongolian-voice-native-review.md) approves it. On **2026-09-29** the PO, who is **one** native reviewer, did a pre-review pass over all 90 rows on the review page: 82 approve, 4 change, 4 unsure. The same day, the PO answered the follow-up questions in chat (relayed by the orchestrator). Those answers settled the 4 unsure rows and the problems the BA flagged in the changed rows. Result: 83 rows `PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending)`, 7 rows `PO-revised 2026-09-29 (panel pending)`, 0 rows `needs native review` (see the change log). Later on 2026-09-29 the BA added the 7 NAV-002 web-demo rows G1–G7 (sections 7 and 8): 6 are `needs native review` and 1 (the ESA WorldCover credit) is `n/a (not translated)`. The PO has not pre-reviewed them. On **2026-09-30** the PO approved «Явах чиглэл дээшээ» (heading up) as the pair of «Хойд зүг дээшээ». It is now its own row in section 5 with status `PO-approved 2026-09-30 (panel pending)`. The PO also confirmed that the old wordings «навигаци», «Төвлөрүүлэх», «км/ц» (as a unit) and the voice ordinal «хоёр дахь» are Avoid (decisions D21 and D22 in [decisions.md](decisions.md)). Also on 2026-09-30 the BA added 3 more NAV-002 web-demo rows in section 5: "Day mode" «Өдрийн горим» and the on-screen units «м» and «км». All 3 are `needs native review`, and the PO has not pre-reviewed them as rows. The pre-review does **not** replace the panel (NAV-007 AC 5–8). Until the panel signs off, use the term in the Mongolian column so there is one consistent string to review and replace. When a term is approved or revised, the BA updates the Status column and the change log, and owners of affected resource files and tests are notified.
> - **Language policy (PO-approved):** app UI, voice, map labels, issue forms, triage comments and PO-facing summaries are Mongolian first (English second). Agent instructions, code, API, commits, ADRs and test plans are in English. Stories and AC are in English and quote user-facing Mongolian exactly, e.g. «300 м-т баруун тийш эргэнэ үү».
> - **Valhalla reference:** "Valhalla" in the Notes column compares with `locales/mn-MN.json` at the pinned version **3.9.0** (ADR-0002). **match** = we use the same wording. **deviate** = we use different wording, and the reason is given.

## 1. Language conventions (apply to every row)
| # | Convention | Rule | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Register of instructions** | Voice and banner instructions use the polite imperative **-на уу / -нэ үү / -но уу / -нө үү**, following vowel harmony: «эргэнэ үү», «явна уу», «орно уу», «гарна уу». Buttons use the verbal noun in **-х**: «Хайх», «Эхлэх», «Дуусгах». | Matches the PO example «300 м-т баруун тийш эргэнэ үү». Valhalla: **deviate**. Valhalla mixes bare imperatives («эргэ», «яв», «гар»), which sound curt, with -аарай («хийгээрэй»), -х («эргэх») and -нэ («эргэнэ»). | PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C2 | **Left/right vs east/west** | In Mongolian, «зүүн» means both *left* and *east*, and «баруун» means both *right* and *west*. **Relative** directions always take «тийш» (or «талаа», «талд», «эгнээ»): «зүүн тийш». **Cardinal** directions always take «зүг»: «зүүн зүг», «хойд зүг». A bare «зүүн»/«баруун» never appears in voice. | Valhalla: **deviate**. Valhalla uses bare «зүүн»/«баруун» for both, so «Зүүн руу чиглүүл.» (start) can be heard as *head left* when it means *head east*. **PO 2026-09-29:** "unsure" (no note) in the review-page pass, then **confirmed as proposed** in the follow-up. The rows that apply the rule ("Turn left / turn right", "Keep left / keep right", "Head <direction>") were already PO-approved. The panel's listening test still checks for **0** left/right and east/west confusions (NAV-007 AC 10). | PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C3 | **Distances** | **Banner:** digits + abbreviation + hyphenated locative, e.g. «300 м-т», «1,5 км-т». **Voice:** the unit is spelled out, e.g. «300 метрт», «1,5 километрт». Voice text never contains «м», «км» or «км/цаг» after a number. Under 10 m: «10 метрээс бага». | Voice avoids abbreviations because TTS may read them letter by letter. Decimal separator: comma («1,5») proposed. The reviewers confirm comma or point. Valhalla: approach alert «<LENGTH>, <CUE>» gives «300 метр, баруун эргэ.» → **deviate** (we add the locative «-т»). «10 метрээс бага» → **match**. | PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C4 | **Ordinals (roundabout exits, khoroo numbers)** | **Banner:** «1-р», «2-р», … (unchanged). **Voice:** spelled out in the **-дугаар / -дүгээр** form, following vowel harmony: «нэгдүгээр», «хоёрдугаар», «гуравдугаар», «дөрөвдүгээр», «тавдугаар», «зургаадугаар», «долоодугаар», «наймдугаар», «есдүгээр», «аравдугаар». Example: «хоёрдугаар гарц». | Valhalla `ordinal_values` «1-р»…«10-р» → **match** for banners, **deviate** for voice, because TTS may read «2-р» as "хоёр эр". The banner «2-р» is the normal abbreviation of «хоёрдугаар», so banner and voice now agree. **PO 2026-09-29:** "unsure" in the review-page pass, note (verbatim) «Нэгдүгээр хоёрдугаар гэж боджийназ» ("I think нэгдүгээр, хоёрдугаар"). **Decided** in the follow-up: voice uses -дугаар/-дүгээр. **Previous proposal:** «эхний», «хоёр дахь», «гурав дахь», «дөрөв дэх», «тав дахь», «зургаа дахь», «долоо дахь», «найм дахь», «ес дэх», «арав дахь». **Avoid** in voice ordinals: «<number> дахь» / «<number> дэх», e.g. «хоёр дахь» (PO confirmed 2026-09-30, D22). «эхний» is not marked Avoid because it has other valid uses; just don't use it as an exit ordinal. | PO-revised 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C5 | **Street and place names in instructions** | Insert names as they are. Avoid hyphen-attached case suffixes on names in voice («<НЭР>-р», «<НЭР>-д»), because the suffix must follow the name's vowel harmony and TTS may read the hyphen. Prefer wording where the name stands alone or takes a separate postposition. The final pattern is set in NAV-007. | Valhalla: **deviate**. Valhalla attaches «-р», «-д», «-с», «-н» with a hyphen and picks «руу»/«рүү» regardless of the name's vowels. | PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C6 | **Time and duration** | Clock time is 24-hour, «14:35». Duration on banners is «1 ц 25 мин». In voice it is «1 цаг 25 минут». | | PO-approved 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C7 | **Speed** | Screen, banner and UI: «60 км/цаг». Voice: «цагт 60 километр». | Avoid «км/ч» (Russian). **PO 2026-09-29:** "unsure" in the review-page pass, note (verbatim) «Км/ц гэдгийг киломерт/цаг» («киломерт» as typed). **Decided** in the follow-up: on screen write «км/цаг» (not «км/ц»); voice stays «цагт 60 километр». **Previous proposal:** banner and UI «60 км/ц». **Avoid** «км/ц» as a unit (PO confirmed 2026-09-30, D22). Scanners must match it only when it is **not** followed by «аг» (regex `км/ц(?!аг)`), because «км/ц» is a prefix of «км/цаг». | PO-revised 2026-09-29 (panel pending) |
| C8 | **Map label and name fallback** | `name:mn` → `name` → `name:en` (CLAUDE.md rule 7). | Applies to voice as well as labels. | n/a (internal) |
