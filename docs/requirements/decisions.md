# Product decision log (owner: business-analyst)

This log records product decisions made by the PO. Each decision has one row and a stable ID (`D<n>`). New decisions continue the numbering. A decision changes only through a new row that names the one it replaces. It is never edited in place.

- **Source of D1–D24:** PO answer on 2026-09-30, relayed in chat by the orchestrator. The PO answered "all recommended", which means the PO accepted the BA or orchestrator recommendation for every question asked. The numbering follows the orchestrator's list.
- **Source of D25–D26:** PO decision on 2026-09-30 in chat, relayed by the orchestrator (hosting provider for NAV-008 staging and the production direction).
- **Source of D27–D33:** PO answer "go ahead" on 2026-09-30, relayed by the orchestrator after NAV-003 verification. D27–D32 answer NAV-003 open questions Q1–Q6 (all as recommended). D33 records the change approvals F1–F5 for NAV-003.
- **Status values:** `decided`, `decided (applied after spike)` (the PO fixed the rule, and the concrete choice follows from measurements), `superseded for <scope> by D<n>`, `open: unanswered`, `open: partly answered`.
- **Information-request rows** (for example D10) are not choices. They get dated additions in place when the PO supplies information. The original text is kept.
- Stories carry the details in their AC, Open questions and Change log. This table is the index.

## Items to confirm (not in the 2026-09-30 list)
These came up while applying the decisions. They are also in the BA handoff under `open_questions`.

1. **NAV-008 Open question 5 (staging data coverage)** is not in the relayed list. "All recommended" would mean (a) Geofabrik `mongolia-latest`. NAV-008 AC 10 and AC 15 already assume it, so the BA treats (a) as the working assumption until the PO confirms.
2. **D4 readiness condition.** The BA recommendation preferred an in-country or own server only if it "can be ready within 10 working days" and meets C1. The relayed D4 names only the RTT condition. Because the PO chose "all recommended", the BA treats the 10-working-day condition as included until the PO says otherwise. **Moot for staging since D25 (2026-09-30).** Whether a readiness deadline applies to production selection is a NAV-009 open question.
3. **D26 vs D9: who may use the Singapore staging before the legal review?** (added 2026-09-30) D9 requires the legal review "before any **outside tester's** traffic goes to a host abroad" (NAV-008 AC 24). The D26 wording says staging is "for testers who know about it" and that the review is required "before any **real user** traffic goes abroad". The relayed text does not say that D9 is replaced. Until the PO says otherwise, the BA keeps D9 and NAV-008 AC 24 **unchanged**: only the project team uses staging until the review outcome is go (or go with conditions). This also means the NAV-007 panel and partner drivers wait for the review. Options for the PO:
   - (a) keep D9 as it is (working assumption)
   - (b) replace D9 with a new row: informed outside testers who have accepted a written tester notice may use staging before the review, and the review is required before real users or production traffic go abroad
   
   BA recommendation: **(a)**. Coordinates from outside testers are still personal data under the 2022 law, and a short counsel review of the tester scenario is cheap next to the risk.
4. **NAV-003 golden row A8 distance** (added 2026-09-30, with D33). F3 said to update A8 to the measured value (374 m against the 300 m limit) "if the architect requested it".
